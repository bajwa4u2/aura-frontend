# Aura 1.4.3 — Store Release Notes (public-facing)

Version `1.4.3+38`, Windows MSIX `1.4.3.0`.

Written to the same shape as `RELEASE_NOTES_1.4.0_STORE.md`: what a person
gets, in the words they would use for it. No internal names, no defect
archaeology, nothing a reader would have to already know Aura to parse.

---

## Short — Play, 500 characters

> Aura now asks for your date of birth and where you are when you create an
> account, so the right age rules apply from the start. Both are private and
> never appear on your profile. If you joined before we asked, nothing changes
> — you keep your account, and we simply invite you to add what we do not know
> yet. Your personal details are now kept separately from your public profile,
> and you can see exactly what Aura has verified about you.

## Full

**Creating an account**

New accounts now give a date of birth and the country whose rules should apply.
Both are private. They are used to apply the right age rules and are never
shown on your profile. Your country is something you tell us — Aura does not
guess it from your connection, your SIM or your device.

**If you already have an account**

Nothing changes for you. You are not asked to re-qualify, and you are never
shut out over facts Aura never collected. Where something is missing, Aura says
so plainly and invites you to add it whenever you like.

**Your details, apart from your profile**

Your legal name, date of birth and country now live in Personal Details,
separate from your public profile. What you show and who you are are two
different things, and changing one does not touch the other. Your sign-in email
is shown there but changed through its own secure flow, not as a profile edit.

**Verification you can actually read**

Aura shows each thing it has verified on its own — who you are, your
institution, and a role or credential — instead of a single badge that could
mean any of them. A person reviews identity documents, not a model, and the
documents are destroyed 60 days after a review ends.

**Smaller repairs**

Your city and country now save correctly on your public profile. Refreshing a
page keeps you signed in and where you were.

---

## What this is NOT

No new permission is requested. No location is read from the device. Nothing
about an existing account's standing changes, and no existing member is asked
to prove anything to keep what they already have.
