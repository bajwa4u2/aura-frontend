# Aura 1.3.0 — Store Release Notes (public-facing)

## Google Play — Release Notes

What's new in this release:

- Track the status of your questions and issues to institutions in one place, from first message to resolution.
- Translate posts and replies across languages, right where you're reading them.
- Better topic suggestions when you post, so your posts reach the right audience.
- Reshares now show your comment alongside the original post.
- Various reliability fixes to posting, replying, notifications, and account activity.

Thank you for using Aura.
