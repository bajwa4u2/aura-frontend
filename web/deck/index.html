Aura · Product brief

Communication that keeps its context.

Aura is a public-first platform for civic discourse and accountable communication: the environment through which people take part in meaningful communication and discourse, and institutions enter under clear identity to build a credible, accountable relationship with the public.

The order

People first. Institutions answer to them.

Most systems for institutional communication start at the institution and hope the public arrives. Aura starts where the need actually starts, and the order is not a presentation choice — it is the product's architecture, and it is frozen.

A person needs to say something, in public, and be answerable for it.

Not to broadcast. To participate, under a clear identity, in a way that can be read back later.

Discourse continues instead of scattering.

A reply belongs to what it answers. A Thread holds context; a Space holds the people who share it. Nothing depends on scrolling back far enough.

What was said stays available and attributable.

Continuity is the property, not an archive feature. The record outlives the moment that produced it.

Public relevance accumulates.

Because participation is durable and attributable, the conversation becomes something an institution cannot afford to be absent from.

The institution enters — under governed identity.

Not as an advertiser. As a participant whose authority to speak is explicit, individually granted, and revocable.

Official speech becomes attributable.

An Announcement carries who authorised it. Correspondence carries who answered. The relationship between the public and the institution can then continue rather than reset.

The reverse order — institutions first, public second — is named and prohibited in the founder-frozen causal doctrine, and a build-breaking test enforces it against Aura's general surfaces.

What is built

Six capability groups, named the way the product names them.

These are the frozen module names, not marketing categories. Each one is running code with a public surface.

Public Discourse

Public Posts, Institution Posts, Replies, and one unified feed that renders every publication class through a single path. Articles are first-class: they translate, they are shared, they are replied to.

Aura Identity

One Person, one identity, and an explicit mode for how they are speaking — personally, as an institution's member, or officially on its behalf. Institutional authority is granted per capability, not all at once.

Correspondence

The governed formal form: a deliberate exchange between a Person and an institution that keeps its thread, its participants, and its outcome. Distinct from casual messaging by design, not by tone.

Institutional Communication

Announcements — a formal institutional statement that carries the authority behind it, published into the same public record everything else lives in.

Meetings & Live

Scheduling, availability and booking, admission control, and self-hosted realtime sessions. Live is a governed mode of an existing Thread or Space, not a separate product bolted on.

Trust & Moderation

Media custody with content scanning, AI provenance and disclosure, moderation that removes distortion rather than disagreement, and an accountability surface that shows its own state.

Identity

Identity is trust infrastructure, not a status symbol.

The identity chip on every publication answers two questions and refuses a third. It says who the author is, and in what capacity they are speaking. It never says how popular they are.

Capacity is explicit

Speaking personally and speaking for an institution are different acts with different consequences. The product never lets them look the same.

Authority is revocable

Who may publish officially, approve, or admit is granted individually and can be withdrawn without dismantling the institution's presence.

No popularity surface

No follower counts, no reach scores, no influencer flair. Standing on Aura comes from what you have said and answered for, not from an audience number.

The record

One accountable record, carried forward.

Context travels with the message

A publication knows what it replied to, which Thread it belongs to, and which Space it was addressed into. Reading it later does not require reconstructing it.

Language is not a wall

Posts and replies translate where they are read, in place, without leaving the conversation or losing attribution to the original author.

Topics route, they do not rank

A publication is placed by what it is about, so it reaches the people it concerns — rather than the people an engagement model predicts will react.

Provenance is recorded

Where AI contributed to a composition, that fact is carried with the publication. Disclosure is a property of the record, not a checkbox at the end.

Governance

A human decision sits inside every consequential action.

Aura uses AI where it helps a person compose, translate, or find what concerns them. It is not permitted to publish, approve, admit, or speak officially. That boundary is enforced at runtime, not stated in a policy document.

Composition is assisted; publication is approved. An institution's official statement passes a human authority check before it exists publicly.
