How it works

One question, from the public to the record.

01 · ASKED

Someone asks, in public

A resident asks why the consultation summary is three weeks late. Anyone can read it.

02 · ARRIVES

It reaches the Desk

The question arrives on the institution's Desk by topic, oldest first, with everything else waiting.

03 · ANSWERED

Answered as the institution

Written beside the question, with what was said before in view, and a due date when it commits.

04 · ON THE RECORD

Kept on the record

The asker sees who answered. The public sees the commitment and whether it was kept.

Questions institutions ask

Before you set up

Who can speak for our institution?

Only people you give a seat: owners, admins and those you grant official voice. Holding a role is not the same as speaking for it; an official answer is a separate act.

How is an institution verified?

By proof that it exists and that the person setting it up has authority, reviewed by a person. Verification is free and never sold, and a plan cannot buy or remove it.

What happens to questions we do not answer?

They stay on your Desk, oldest first, and you are reminded. Your public record shows what you answered and the commitments you made, including any that are overdue.

Do people pay anything?

No. Aura is free for the public, permanently, including translation, AI writing help and calls, within a daily fair use.

Which languages work?

Every script, right to left included. A post can be read in the reader's own language, with the original kept.
